Get a working local world (built from sources):
1) In pycharm, open the "built APLauncher" by typing in the terminal: py .\Launcher.py
2) Launch "Build APWorlds"
3) Double-clic the <apworld>.apworld output or copy it in <AP folder>/custom_world/
4) Open the regular ArchipelagoLauncher.exe, click "Generate Template Options"
5) Put the generated template from Archipelago/Players/Templates/ to Archipelago/Players/ and edit it manually
6) In the regular ArchipelagoLauncher.exe, click "Generate" to setup a game with the yaml in the Archipelago/ouput/ folder
7) The game can now be hosted with "Host" in the Archipelago launcher by using the generated game in Archipelago/output/
